Investment & market entry

Feasibility & CAPEX analysis

Independent evaluation of market entry feasibility, projected ROI, baseline financial modeling, and realistic budget estimation.

Technology & supplier selection

Risk mitigation strategy

02 / 06

Production process

The recipe, the raw material and the thermal profile that make a granulate consistent.

Raw material qualification

Testing and selecting suitable post-consumer or industrial waste glass cullet sources to ensure consistent melting points and expanding properties.

Recipe & batch formulation

Developing custom agent and additive formulations fine-tuned for precise density, crushing strength, and grain size distribution.

Thermal & process optimization

Engineering precise temperature profiles and energy-efficient kiln configurations to ensure high yield with minimal power consumption.

03 / 06

Quality control systems

Batch-to-batch consistency that customers and certification bodies can rely on.

Process standard deployment

Implementing a strict quality assurance framework covering raw cullet inspection, continuous temperature monitoring, and final product density and strength verification.

Lab protocol setup

Equipping and configuring on-site testing procedures to maintain batch-to-batch consistency and international product certification readiness.

Real-time monitoring architecture

Establishing standard operating parameters that allow operators to spot and correct process deviations immediately.

04 / 06

Plant commissioning & start-up

From layout to designed output — safely and with minimal off-spec product.

End-to-end layout & engineering oversight

Advisory on optimal equipment placement, airflow, batch transport, and material handling for seamless factory throughput.

Trial runs & commissioning support

Hands-on supervision during cold and hot commissioning phases to achieve designed production volume safely.

Yield & waste reduction

Fine-tuning parameters during start-up to minimize off-spec granulate and operational waste.

05 / 06

Staff training

A team that can run the line without outside help.

Standard operating procedures

Custom creation of clear operational guides, emergency protocols, and preventive maintenance routines.

Hands-on team coaching

Practical training for engineers, shift supervisors, and line operators based on real-world plant operations.

Safety & maintenance protocols

Establishing routines for critical wear-and-tear components to avoid unplanned facility downtime.

06 / 06

Ongoing advisory & support

Continued improvement after the plant is running.

Performance optimization

Periodic audits to reduce unit production costs, lower thermal energy consumption, and boost throughput.

Process deviation diagnostics

Rapid troubleshooting support for unexpected product defects, density shifts, or equipment inefficiency.

R&D & product expansion

Guidance on introducing new product lines, e.g. ultra-lightweight aggregates or specialized acoustic and thermal insulation fillers.
