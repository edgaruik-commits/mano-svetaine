How we work

A clear path from idea to output

Each phase ends with a concrete deliverable, so investment decisions are made on evidence rather than assumptions.

Feasibility

Market, raw material and CAPEX assessment with a realistic budget and ROI model.

Technology

Equipment selection and supplier evaluation sized to your target output.

Process

Raw material qualification, recipe and thermal profile development.

Commissioning

Layout oversight, cold and hot commissioning, ramp-up to designed volume.

Operation

SOPs, staff training, quality systems and ongoing optimization.

Experience

Hands-on experience inside a working plant, not a case study

Competence across the entire value chain — from cullet processing to export sales.

Engineering

Recycled glass cullet processing, glass milling technology, drying process, lightweight aggregate screening, raw material selection, equipment specification, and full production line and plant design — from concept to a working factory layout.

Production

Raw material supplier qualification, formula development, furnace and equipment parameter setting, and full production process control and record-keeping.

Quality control

Incoming raw material quality checks, in-process quality standards, and preparation of quality control protocols and documentation.

Marketing & sales
