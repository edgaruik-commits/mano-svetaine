About us

Why EGA exists

Expanded Glass Advisory was founded to open up expanded glass granulate production to companies looking to invest in and develop businesses in this high-margin, high-entry-barrier sector.

Built on direct experience inside an operating production facility and backed by applied research — rather than limited to a pure lab setting — EGA ensures every recommendation is grounded in practical, science-based manufacturing that delivers reliable performance at scale, day after day.

We work independently of equipment suppliers. Our only interest is that your plant is built right, starts on time and produces granulate that meets specification.

Experience

Hands-on experience inside a working plant, not a case study

Engineering

Recycled glass cullet processing, glass milling technology, drying process, lightweight aggregate screening, raw material selection, equipment specification, and full production line and plant design — from concept to a working factory layout.

Production

Raw material supplier qualification, formula development, furnace and equipment parameter setting, and full production process control and record-keeping.

Quality control

Incoming raw material quality checks, in-process quality standards, and preparation of quality control protocols and documentation.

Marketing & sales

Export strategy built around the largest global B2B markets, with a client base of international companies operating at scale.
